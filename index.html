Your next 90 days — based on your two scores

This plan changes as you move the sliders. It's sequenced: stabilise first, build runway second, switch third — in that order if burnout is high.

How the scores work: Burnout (0–100) = Workload (max 40: weekly hours + after-hours + weekends) + Commute drain (max 25: daily commute time) + Recovery deficit (max 35: sleep shortfall + no exercise + no leave + Sunday dread). Runway = touchable savings ÷ bare-minimum monthly spend. Target date assumes you keep saving your current surplus (take-home − current spend) every month.

This is a planning tool, not medical or financial advice. If you're feeling burnt out persistently — exhaustion, sleep problems, anxiety that doesn't lift on weekends — talk to a doctor or counsellor. In India you can call Tele-MANAS on 14416 for free mental-health support.
